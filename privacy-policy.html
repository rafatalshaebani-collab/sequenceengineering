Sequence Engineering Pty Ltd (ABN 98 694 041 864, "Sequence Engineering", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through sequenceengineering.com.au, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, area of interest and project details when you use our contact form. Our contact form opens your email client and sends this information directly to us by email; we do not store form submissions on our servers.

We also collect standard technical and usage information automatically through Google Analytics, including pages visited, approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser type, and referring website.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide requested information about our services
  • To understand how visitors use our website and improve its content and performance
  • To meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to help us understand aggregate visitor behaviour. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of Google Analytics using Google's browser add-on.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our website and business (such as hosting and analytics providers), or where required by law.

Data storage and security

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website, including the use of HTTPS encryption for all traffic.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Principles (Privacy Act 1988), you may request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you, or ask us to explain how it has been used. Contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy
